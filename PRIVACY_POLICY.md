# Privacy Policy for Glance PDF

**Last Updated:** July 4, 2026

At Glance PDF, we are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our application handles your data.

## 1. No Personal Data Collection
Glance PDF does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information, usage analytics, or device identifiers. 

## 2. Local Processing of PDF Files
All PDF processing, rendering, and annotation saving are performed entirely locally on your device. Your documents, notes, highlights, and drawings never leave your computer and are not transmitted to any external servers or third parties.

## 3. Storage of Annotations
When you create notes or highlights, they are stored locally on your device in the application's local folder. You have full control over these files, and they can be deleted at any time by removing your annotations or uninstalling the application.

## 4. Internet Access
Glance PDF may require internet access solely to resolve external hyperlinks contained within your PDF documents when you click on them (which will open in your default system web browser) or to check for application updates via the Microsoft Store. We do not track or monitor your browsing activity.

## 5. Contact Information
If you have any questions or feedback about this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer through our GitHub repository:
[https://github.com/jonas1ara/Glance](https://github.com/jonas1ara/Glance)
